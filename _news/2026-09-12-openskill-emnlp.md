**OpenSkill: Open-World Self-Evolution for LLM Agents** was accepted to the **EMNLP 2026 Main Conference**. Led by Zhiling Yan, OpenSkill asks whether an agent can evolve given nothing but a task prompt — no curated skills, no successful trajectories, no verifier. It builds both its skills and its own supervision from documentation and repositories, anchoring self-built tests in independently verifiable facts rather than target answers. On SkillsBench it raises the overall pass rate from 25.5% to 43.6%.
