Posted **Metis AI: The Overlooked Middle Zone Between AI-Native and World-Movers** (arXiv:2605.14407). The dominant framing puts the boundary of AI capability between digital and physical tasks. I argue the more consequential boundary runs *within* digital work: a wide class of tasks that are computationally accessible yet resist automation because they are institutionally, socially, and normatively entangled.
