**UniBrain: An End-to-End Unified Toolkit for Comprehensive Neuroimaging Analysis** has received an **NIH/NIBIB R01**. Lifang He (Lehigh) serves as Contact PI, leading a multi-institutional team across Lehigh, MGH, Emory, and the University of Pittsburgh, with Carl Yang and Liang Zhan. UniBrain aims at a framework for more integrated, reproducible, and scalable neuroimaging analysis.
