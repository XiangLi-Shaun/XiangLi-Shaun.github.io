New work on **physician trust in agentic AI**, heading to **KDD**: across treatment-planning cases, three physicians trusted the system's clinical reasoning in **89.57%** of instances. The premise is that trust — not accuracy — is the real barrier to clinical adoption, because a reasoning model's narrated logic is self-reported and anchored to nothing a physician can independently check. Preprint at arXiv:2606.30658.
