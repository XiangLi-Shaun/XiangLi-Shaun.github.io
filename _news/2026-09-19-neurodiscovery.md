In 2016 I set out a ten-year roadmap in *Functional Neuroimaging in the New Era of Big Data*: turn neuroimaging big data into knowledge, and build informatics able to share it at that scale. This year the first half arrived — our fMRI foundation model in *Nature Biomedical Engineering* — and we have now launched **NeuroDiscovery**, an agentic AI harness that runs end-to-end neuroimaging research continuously. With NIBIB support, and as co-PI with Lifang He, the second half is next.
